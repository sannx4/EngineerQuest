# Research plan and evidence boundaries

No interviews have been conducted and no participants have been contacted by this batch.
Recruitment requires real participants; no invented quotations or findings are included.

## Interview script (25 minutes)

Obtain consent for notes; no recording by default. Ask: describe your last difficult
engineering task; how did you practice; what failed; what made you return; show a recent
learning session if comfortable; try the prototype without coaching; what would make
you abandon it? Ask behavior questions before introducing product concepts.

Store de-identified notes with participant ID, consent scope, date, exact observation,
interpretation and confidence. Keep contact details outside Git. Delete raw notes after
90 days; honor withdrawal. Recruit five adults across junior engineers and career switchers.

## Hypotheses to test

1. Learners cannot transfer tutorial knowledge to unfamiliar tasks: 3/5 describe a recent example.
2. Useful failure feedback increases willingness to retry: 4/5 retry unaided in prototype.
3. A visible prerequisite path reduces choice overload: 4/5 choose first lesson within 60 seconds.

These are proposed pains, not ranked findings. Revise personas only after synthesis.
